# Code of conduct

PlaceContext is committed to a welcoming, constructive community.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make room for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained
disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior conflicts with
these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainers or
security reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
